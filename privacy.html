Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers https://c0dewars.github.io/, the personal portfolio operated by Bruno Garcia in Porto, Portugal. For privacy questions or requests, contact Bruno through LinkedIn or the GitHub profile. Please do not post personal information in public repository issues.

Hosting and essential processing

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Your browser sends technical information needed to deliver pages and assets, including your IP address. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security, even when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its processing, retention and international transfer practices. Providing the website securely is our legitimate interest.

Optional Google Analytics

Only if you accept analytics, this website loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-DS2PN2ZKER) to understand visits and improve the portfolio. Consent is the basis for this optional processing. Before acceptance, or after rejection, this implementation does not load the Google tag or send analytics pings to Google.

After acceptance, Google receives page and usage events, timestamps, referral information, browser and device information, and cookie identifiers. Your IP address is transmitted as part of the connection; Google states that GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. Query strings and URL fragments are removed from the initial page URL and referrer supplied by this site's code. Google may collect additional events depending on the property's enhanced measurement settings.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation consent remain denied. This site's tag disables Google signals and advertising personalisation signals. It does not set a user ID. The Google Analytics property's own settings are managed separately.

Cookies and your choice

The localStorage entry analytics-consent-v1 remembers your acceptance or rejection for 180 days. It is used only to honour your privacy choice. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice may apply only to the current page.

After acceptance, Google Analytics may set _ga and _ga_DS2PN2ZKER cookies to distinguish browsers and maintain session information. This tag sets a 180-day cookie lifetime without renewing it on each visit. You may delete cookies sooner in your browser.

Use in the footer or here to accept or reject at any time. Rejection after acceptance disables this measurement ID, deletes this site's accessible _ga cookies and reloads the page to unload Google Analytics. Withdrawal does not undo processing already performed. With JavaScript disabled, analytics does not run.

Providers, transfers and retention

Google provides Analytics through Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area; processing may involve Google LLC in the United States and other locations. See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google's data processing terms, including applicable transfer safeguards.

The 180-day periods above concern browser storage, not server-side report retention. Analytics event retention and other data settings are controlled in the Google Analytics property; this website does not publish an unverified account-specific retention period. GitHub and Google retain data according to their applicable settings, policies and obligations.

Contact links and your rights

This site has no contact form or account system. Following an external profile link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices. Information you send there is handled by that service and used by Bruno to respond to your enquiry.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time. Some requests depend on the data held and legal conditions. You may complain to Portugal's Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) or your local supervisory authority.
